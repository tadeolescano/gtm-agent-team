# Example: veterinary clinic reminders

> Fictional example. Company names, prices and figures are invented for illustration.

## Input (via router)

> Small veterinary clinics with terrible appointment reminders. Something to sell them with AI?

## Output

**Top idea: Messaging-app vaccine and check-up reminders, done for you**

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Customer | Independent vet clinics, 1 to 3 vets, no practice-management add-ons |
| Pain | "Owners forget boosters; we call them one by one when we have time, which is never." |
| Offer | Import the client list once, set up automated reminders 7 days and 1 day before due dates, a weekly no-show report. Not included: a booking system, a new CRM. |
| Price and model | Setup 250 + 59/month `[ASSUMPTION]`, to be tested in the experiment |
| Cost and hours | ~6 h setup, ~1 h/month `[ESTIMATE]` 5 to 8 h based on two similar CSV-import builds |
| First 10 customers | Walk-in visits to clinics within 30 min, with a printed one-page before/after |
| Risks | 1) Messaging-platform template approval delays. 2) Client data export varies by clinic software. 3) Low willingness to pay below 2 vets. |
| Viability | 7/10: clear pain, easy demo, weak defensibility |
| 7-day experiment | Visit 15 clinics with the one-pager. **Success: 3 agree to a paid setup.** Below 2: kill. |

**Killed ideas**

| Idea | Reason |
|---|---|
| AI symptom-checker chatbot for pet owners | Regulated advice risk; clinics will not put their name on it |
| Review-generation automation | Crowded; existing tools sell it at 20/month `[FACT]` (competitor pricing pages, fictional) |
