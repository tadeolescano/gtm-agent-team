# Example: learning from feedback

> Fictional example.

## Input

> Idea 2 needs a custom integration per client. I don't want anything that takes more than two weeks to set up.

## Output

> learned: nothing that needs more than two weeks of setup.
>
> Idea 2 killed for that reason and logged. Idea 3 (shared inbox triage for accounting firms) also needed per-client integrations; killed too. Two replacements below, both under one week of setup.

The rule is saved to the agent's persistent notes and applied to every later request.
